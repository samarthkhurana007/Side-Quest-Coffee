Come for the coffee. Stay for the game.

Side Quest Coffee Co. is a neighborhood coffee shop where tabletop games are a big part of why people stay. You were on your way somewhere else and ended up having an unexpectedly good afternoon.

Grab a drink, pull a game off the shelf, and sit at the big community table. Board games, cards, dice, and chess are all here, and people actually look at each other instead of a screen for a while.

You don't have to be a board-game person

Some of our customers play games every week. Some haven't played anything since Monopoly when they were ten. Both are welcome here. Students, game groups, remote workers, neighborhood regulars, parents, and people meeting for work all find a spot, among warm wood tables, mismatched chairs, and plants in the windows.

Events

Open Table Thursday

Every Thursday, 6:30 to 9:30 PM. Bring your own games or grab something from our shelves. It's fine to come alone.

Chess & Coffee

Every Sunday, 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM. It's casual, and you absolutely do not have to be good at chess.

Learn-a-Game Night

The first Wednesday of each month, 7:00 to 8:30 PM. A staff member picks a game and teaches it. It's a good fit if you like the idea of tabletop games but don't know where to start.
